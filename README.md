# TRIPLE-A-TAILORING-SERVICES
A modern tailoring service for all kinds of attire in the heart of Dar es Salaaam in Tanzania
TRIPLE A TAILORING SERVICES with its Head Office at the 4th Floor of the Kisutu Central Market along Bibi Titi Road in Dar Es Salaam, is a local Tanzanian firm legally registered by Tanzania Legal Authorities. This firm was re-registered on 17th April 2013 after operating since 2006 under the name of Hidaya Fashions. After re-registration it was presented with the certificate of registration No.263364 issued by the Business Registration and Licensing Agency (BRELA). 
TRIPLE A TAILORING SERVICES’s Mission is “to always work hard and intelligently to elevate the local fashion design industry to international standards and make sure that our customers whether individuals or organizations have the best that can be obtained from any world class designer.” We, at TRIPLE A TAILORING SERVICES’s, take seriously customer satisfaction and we go to great lengths to ensure that our customers’ expectations are attended to in the most professional manner. To us, a customer is not merely a King but a Partner who provides us with the opportunity to learn and grow and in the process develop a mutually beneficial business relationship.
Our Firms offers the following products: Clothing Line 1.Male and Female custom made executive three-piece suits;2.Male and  Female Custom-made executive safari suits; 3.Designer shirts & ladies’ blouses; 4.Male and Female designer trousers; 5.Designer jackets; 6.Bridal attires; 7.Children clothing; 8.Evening clothing; 9.Uniforms for security guards, hospital personnel, industrial workers, office, sales   and customer center workers;  10.Custom made T-shirts designing and printing; and 11.Other custom-made garments.
