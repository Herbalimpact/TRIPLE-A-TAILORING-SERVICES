The Triple A Tailoring Services Story

From Hidaya Fashions in 2006 to Triple A Tailoring Services (TATS) today — years of quality tailoring experience in Dar es Salaam.

Our History

Our History

TRIPLE A TAILORING SERVICES, with its Head Office on the 4th Floor of Kisutu Central Market along Bibi Titi Mohammed Road in Dar es Salaam, is a local Tanzanian firm legally registered by Tanzanian authorities.

The firm was re-registered on 17th April 2013 after operating since 2006 under the name Hidaya Fashions. After re-registration it was issued certificate of registration No. 263364 by the Business Registration and Licensing Agency (BRELA).
